Walmart Store Sales Forecasting & Analysis

Analyzed 421,570 weekly sales records across 45 stores (2010-2012) to identify what drives store-level revenue. Built a Random Forest Regression model to forecast weekly sales with a 94.5% R² score.

Key Findings
Type A stores (avg 182K sq ft) generated 64.3% of total revenue — $4.33B of $6.73B total
Holiday weeks drove 7.13% higher sales ($17,035 vs $15,901 avg weekly)
Markdowns produced only 1.92% sales lift — raising questions about promotional ROI
Random Forest model: 94.5% R², $2,271 MAE — department was top predictor at 0.61 importance
Python scikit-learn pandas Random Forest Tableau MySQL
